# DraftOps Roadshow PPT Design Spec

## I. Project Brief
- Deck title: DraftOps: 面向 2026 NBA 选秀的 Agent 预测驾驶舱
- Output: 8-page, 16:9 roadshow deck for a 6-minute competition presentation.
- Audience: AWS/NBA competition judges, technical reviewers, and NBA data specialists.
- Content boundary: explain system, method, AWS/RAG usage, verification, and deliverables without manually asserting any player replacement or hidden prediction change.
- Source truth: local README, PPT制作要点.md, grounding audit report, AWS RAG sync report, and live product screenshots.

## VIII. Screenshot Preservation Manifest
The product screenshots are high-complexity UI rasters. They are preserved as raster evidence per xiaobei-skill hybrid preservation rules; surrounding titles, labels, captions, arrows, and layout structure are generated as editable SVG/PPT objects.

## IX. Notes
- No API key, account secret, private token, or private credential appears in the deck.
- Internal scoring weights are summarized as factors rather than exposed as tuning controls.
- Player picks are described as locked board outputs generated by Agent/script artifacts, not manual edits.
