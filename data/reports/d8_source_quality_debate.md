| Combine measurements and athletic testing | User-provided official combine history package |

Rule: if a media source conflicts with an official value, keep the official value, lower the affected media-signal confidence, and add a review note instead of averaging the conflict.

## 5. Media-Coverable Fields

Media sources may cover predictive or interpretive fields, but only with source-tier weighting and deduplication.

| Field family | Allowed media evidence | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consensus/mock rank | NBA.com consensus, ESPN, CBS, Yahoo, The Ringer, The Athletic, Bleacher | Do not double-count NBA.com consensus and every underlying mock as fully independent unless the aggregation method explicitly requires it. |
| Big-board rank and prospect tier | ESPN primary; NBC/Bleacher/Ringer support | Treat each outlet family once unless author-level weighting is implemented. |
| Team fit and roster need | ESPN final mock, CBS article mocks, The Ringer guide, The Athletic via NBA.com | Fit is weaker than factual/player-quality evidence; cap its influence. |
| Player strengths, risks, archetype, readiness, upside | Major media scouting notes and draft guides | Use as opinion signals. Require official facts for measurable claims. |
| Draft movement and late momentum | Current/final major mocks and NBA.com consensus | Same-outlet older mocks become historical movement evidence, not current truth. |
| Future pick context | RealGM plus official order | Field-limited to pick obligations/trade provenance. |

## 6. Cross-Validation Rules

1. Official beats all non-official sources for factual fields.
2. For prediction fields, require at least two approved sources before assigning high confidence unless the signal is from NBA.com consensus.
3. Treat same-outlet items as correlated. Two CBS authors are useful disagreement evidence, but not equal to two fully independent outlets.
4. Treat NBA.com consensus as an aggregation source. If the model also ingests ESPN/CBS/Yahoo/Ringer/Athletic directly, apply a correlation cap so consensus does not double-count the same underlying market.
5. Prefer fresher same-outlet sources. In the current inventory, ESPN final mock should override ESPN post-combine mock for current pick projections; the older mock remains movement history.
6. When approved media disagree sharply, lower confidence rather than forcing a false average. The model should represent uncertainty.
7. Cross-source agreement can raise confidence but should not raise source quality beyond the maximum tier coefficient. A medium source confirmed by a high source becomes supporting evidence, not a high source by itself.

Recommended cross-validation confidence adjustment:

| Evidence pattern | Adjustment |
| --- | ---: |
| Official source present and no conflict | `+0.08` confidence cap boost |
| Three or more independent A-tier media agree | `+0.05` prediction confidence |
| One A-tier plus one B-tier agree | `+0.03` prediction confidence |
| Same-outlet duplicate only | `+0.00`; deduplicate |
| Approved sources conflict | `-0.05` to `-0.12`, depending on spread |
| Any value relies only on C/D/E tier | Do not use as final value |

## 7. Low-Trust Source Handling

| Source class | Handling |
| --- | --- |

| Official outlet YouTube | Context-only fallback. Prefer the article page. If used, transcript must be reviewed and must not duplicate the same outlet's article signal. |
| Self-media YouTube | Ignore. If it references ESPN/Yahoo/CBS, fetch and evaluate the original article instead. |
| Reddit/community posts | Ignore for rankings, confidence, and roster facts. Use only as a pointer to original sources. |
| Facebook/Instagram reposts | Ignore. Reposts do not establish source credibility or freshness. |
| Social buzz | No model input. Do not convert social volume into momentum, heat, or intel. |

| Signal | D8 weight | Source-quality reason |

| `intel` | 8 | Late intel and movement are valuable only when sourced to current major outlets. Cap because provenance and outlet correlation are uneven. |

```json
  "need": 18,
  "player": 27,
  "upside": 15,
  "intel": 9
}
```

This fallback reduces overcounting risk by shifting a small amount from consensus into official/player evidence.

## 9. Final D8 Ruling

1. Official/user-provided sources are the only authority for roster membership, official bio fields, draft order, historical draft outcomes, combine measurements, and metric definitions.
2. Major professional media can enter the model for prediction and evaluation fields: mock rank, big board, tier, team fit, role, risk, upside, and movement.
3. Secondary media and aggregators can support or break ties, but cannot override official facts or dominate major-media consensus.
4. Cross-validation should raise or lower confidence, not rewrite official facts.
5. Social media, community posts, self-media video, reposts, and Wikipedia should not feed the model. Their only valid use is source discovery, followed by verification against approved sources.
6. The recommended D8 scoring stance is `34/18/26/14/8` for `consensus/need/player/upside/intel`, with source-tier multipliers applied inside each signal.
