## 3. How Age Should Add Or Subtract Value

Recommended scoring stance:

- Add age points only when age creates extra development runway and the profile already has NBA tools.
- Do not give full age credit to low-feel or medically risky prospects without a risk gate.
- Subtract age points when an older player is being valued for future growth instead of current role value.
- Move older productive players into a maturity/readiness lane: shooting, defense, feel, size, and role clarity.

## 4. Upside And Risk Tolerance Interaction

Current code adds a small risk-tolerance premium:

```ts
riskPremium = (team.riskTolerance - 50) * 0.16
```

That means a very risk-tolerant team at 86 gets only about +5.8 upside points, while a low-risk team at 42 gets only about -1.3. This is too weak to express real front-office behavior.

Recommended interaction:

| Team risk tolerance | Upside behavior | Risk behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| < 50 | Downweight upside by 4-6 effective points. Require readiness floor. Multiply non-medical risk by about 1.15-1.25. |
| 50-69 | Baseline. Let upside break ties only when player grade is close. |
| 70-84 | Add 3-5 effective upside points. Allow younger / lower-readiness profiles if tools are strong. Reduce non-medical risk slightly. |
| >= 85 | Add 5-7 effective upside points for rebuild/asset teams. Still do not waive medical or source-quality flags. |

The important rule: risk tolerance should change both the reward and the penalty.

For example:
- Washington, Brooklyn, and Oklahoma City can use upside to beat narrow team fit.
- Philadelphia, New York, Denver, Boston, Minnesota, and Cleveland should require cleaner readiness or role certainty.
- Medical, source-quality, and generated-profile risks should have a minimum penalty even for high-risk teams.

## 5. Senior And International Treatment

### Senior

Senior should mean "maturity evidence", not "automatic downgrade".

Recommended treatment:

- Add readiness value when a Senior has high feel, defense, shooting, or a clean role.
- Cap upside when age is 22+ unless the player has rare physical tools.
- Make Senior prospects more attractive for low-risk contenders and less attractive for rebuild teams chasing star outcomes.
- Do not let center/need scarcity lift a Senior with weak data-quality support.

## 9. Final Parameter Stance

Recommended debate position:

1. Replace the smooth current age formula with a segmented development-window curve.
2. Keep age inside upside, but add a separate maturity/readiness interpretation for older players.
3. Let high-risk teams buy upside, but keep medical/source-quality penalties alive for everyone.
4. Treat Senior as floor evidence and International as pathway/scarcity evidence, not as automatic plus/minus labels.
5. Use `30/20/24/18/8` for D6 age/upside mode, or `31/21/24/16/8` if the code-level age curve is not changed.

The board should become more honest about why a player is being lifted: young runway, scarce archetype, mature readiness, or team risk appetite. Mixing those into one generic upside score is the main thing D6 should prevent.
