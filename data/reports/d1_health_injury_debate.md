## 1. D1 Position

Health should be modeled as a separate availability/medical-confidence factor, not mixed into `player` or `upside`. A healthy prospect should not get a major bonus just because no injury is public; the factor should mainly prevent consensus and upside from over-lifting players with public medical uncertainty.

Recommended model behavior:

| Rule | D1 recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Factor name | `health_availability` |
| Direction | 100 = clean public availability; lower = more medical, recurrence, or absence risk |
| Use | Apply as a capped negative modifier inside the final score, not as a full talent rerank |
| Missing data | Treat as neutral-to-uncertain, not proof of health |
| Source gating | Official/team source or major media required; social/repost claims ignored |

Suggested 100-point health score:

| Sub-factor | Weight | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Injury severity / body area | 30 | ACL/MCL/meniscus, chronic back, repeated ankle, and big-man lower-body issues matter more than short illness or isolated hand injuries. |
| Recency and clearance | 20 | Injury in final months or pre-draft process gets a lower score unless full-contact return is documented. |
| Games missed / availability | 20 | Multi-game absences, missed tournament games, or repeated mid-game exits should be penalized. |
| Recurrence pattern | 15 | Same body area, multiple absences, or unresolved diagnosis carries extra risk. |
| Return proof | 10 | Full-minute game return, postseason workload, or official no-restriction update reduces discount. |
| Evidence confidence | 5 | Clear official/ESPN reporting scores higher than ambiguous "undisclosed" reports. |
