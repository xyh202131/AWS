# 2026 NBA Draft Order: Picks 1-60

Washington won the Draft Lottery and will have the first pick in the 2026 NBA Draft (June 23-24). Here is the offical 1-60 order of selection.

NBA.com Staff

Updated on June 4, 2026 8:43 AM

Watch the full 2026 NBA Draft Lottery drawing from Chicago that resulted in the Washington Wizards receiving the No. 1 overall pick.

The order of selection for the first 14 picks of the **[2026 NBA Draft](https://web.archive.org/web/20250118042223mp_/https://www.nba.com/draft/2026)** was determined by the [2026 NBA Draft Lottery](https://www.nba.com/news/2026-nba-draft-lottery-result) on May 10, with the Washington Wizards, Utah Jazz, Memphis Grizzlies and Chicago Bulls securing the first four picks.

The remaining “lottery teams” are positioned five through 14, in reverse order of their 2025-26 regular-season records.

The complete order of selection of the first round (No. 15-30) and the entire second round (No. 31-60) is determined by reverse order of regular-season record. Each NBA team receives one selection in the first round and one selection in the second round.

Here’s the complete order for the 2026 NBA Draft.
